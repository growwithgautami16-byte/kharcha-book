KHARCHA BOOK: SETUP (about 25 minutes, on your MacBook)

A. FIREBASE: your free login and storage
1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with your Gmail.
2. Create a project named "kharcha-book". Turn Google Analytics off.
3. Build > Authentication > Get started > Email/Password > Enable > Save.
4. Authentication > Users > Add user: add your email and a password.
   Add Akash's email and a password too.
5. Build > Firestore Database > Create database.
   Location: asia-south1 (Mumbai). Start in production mode.
6. Firestore > Rules tab: delete everything, paste the contents of
   firestore.rules (put Akash's email in place of AKASH_EMAIL_HERE), Publish.
7. Project settings (gear icon) > Your apps > Web (</> icon).
   Name it "Kharcha Book", leave Firebase Hosting unticked, Register.
   Copy the values inside firebaseConfig.

B. CONFIG
8. Open config.js in TextEdit and paste the six values over each "PASTE_HERE".
   Replace AKASH_EMAIL_HERE with Akash's email. Save.
   (Or send Claude the firebaseConfig and Akash's email, and Claude
   will fill it in and send back a ready folder.)

C. PUT IT ONLINE (Netlify, free)
9.  Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign up with your Gmail.
10. Drag the whole kharcha-app folder onto the page. Wait for "Published".
11. Site configuration > Change site name, for example "kharcha-akash-gautami".
    Your app is now at https://kharcha-akash-gautami.netlify.app
    To update later, open the site > Deploys > drag the folder again.

D. ON BOTH IPHONES
12. Open the link in Safari (it must be Safari), sign in.
13. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
    Open Kharcha from the home screen from now on. It opens full screen
    and stays signed in.

Costs: Firebase Spark plan and Netlify free plan are both Rs 0 for two people.
